I am a PhD candidate in Neuroscience at UCL and Imperial College London. With a background in pharmacology, I am interested in how molecular perturbations, like drugs, can be used to generate a multiscale understanding of the human brain, tracing causal lines from microscopic cellular circuits to macroscopic networks and collective function. Broadly, my research aims to chart how distributed computation gives rise to emergent phenomena, by mapping fundamental modes of information dynamics. In doing so, I try to advance theory-driven markers of global brain state, as well as data-driven approaches that guide new theory.
